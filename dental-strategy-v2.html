Bedside Kit
Floss picks + CloSYS bottle + discard cup
  1. Floss — String floss or water flosser
  2. Rinse — CloSYS Ultra Sensitive, 30–60 sec
  3. Brush — 2 min with Twice Spearmint Eucalyptus, scrape tongue
Try to wait 20–30 min after eating before brushing — food acids temporarily soften enamel. If you need to brush right after eating, swish a pinch of baking soda in water for 30 sec first to neutralize. On weekdays this isn't an issue since you don't eat breakfast at home.
⏰ Teeth brushed by midnight
No matter what. Guarantees a 30-min buffer before your 12:30am smoke.
Dinner → wait 30 min → brush → projects → smoke 12:30+ → bedside rinse
Post-dinner brush
  1. Floss — String floss, then water flosser for gum pockets
  2. Rinse — CloSYS Ultra Sensitive, 30–60 sec
  3. Brush — 2 min with SuperMouth Hydroxamin, scrape tongue
You're clean and mineralized. SuperMouth gets hours of contact time while you work on projects — better for enamel repair than brushing right before sleep.
After smoke, from bed
  1. CloSYS Rinse — Swig, swish 60 sec, gargle, spit into discard cup
  2. Floss Pick (optional) — If you have the energy, hit the tight spots
Woke up on couch → bathroom → full routine → smoke → bedside rinse
  1. Floss — String floss or water flosser
  2. Rinse — CloSYS Ultra Sensitive, 30–60 sec
  3. Brush — 2 min with SuperMouth Hydroxamin, scrape tongue
  4. Wait 15 min — Let enamel remineralize before smoking
  5. Smoke → CloSYS rinse from bed
Aim for 15 min between brushing and smoking — most enamel remineralization has settled by then. But even with zero wait, brushing is infinitely better than not brushing. Twice in the morning lifts whatever extra stain sets overnight.
Woke up on couch → bathroom → quick brush + rinse → smoke → bedside rinse
  1. Brush — 2 min with SuperMouth Hydroxamin
  2. Rinse — CloSYS Ultra Sensitive, 30–60 sec
  3. Smoke → CloSYS rinse from bed
On low-energy couch nights, brush + rinse is your floor. Even without flossing or tongue scraping, you're removing the day's plaque before smoking. That alone makes a huge difference.
